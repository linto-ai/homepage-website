Questions

Is LinTO really free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you one hour of transcription per week, five AI summaries, twenty chat messages and a one-time live credit, with the full editor. No credit card. The code itself is free software, so you can also host it yourself.

Where is my data hosted?

LinTO Studio Cloud is hosted in France by LINAGORA. Your files and transcripts are not used to train models and are not shared with third parties. For stricter needs, the same platform runs on your own infrastructure.

Which languages are supported?

File transcription covers more than 90 languages. Live transcription supports up to 140 languages depending on the engine, with simultaneous translation into several languages at once.

Can LinTO join my Teams meetings?

Yes. A LinTO bot joins Microsoft Teams, Jitsi, BigBlueButton and Visio (La Suite numérique) meetings, transcribes them live with speaker names, and the transcript lands in your workspace. Google Meet and Zoom are not supported yet.

I organize a conference. What do I need?

An audio feed per room from your AV control room (SRT or RTMP), and a screen or a QR code for attendees. We handle the rest, or we set your team up to run it. See the live events page and request a quote.
