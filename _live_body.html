How it works

Three pieces. Your audio, our engine, your audience.

Plug in the audio

One SRT or RTMP stream per room from your AV control room, a microphone in a browser, or a bot that joins your Microsoft Teams, Jitsi or BigBlueButton meeting. Hybrid events mix all three.

LinTO transcribes and translates

Each room gets its own channel with its language settings. Speech is transcribed in up to 140 languages and translated into as many target languages as you need, sentence by sentence, without flicker.

Show it to the audience

A public page per session, reached by QR code or link, where each attendee picks their language, subtitles or full transcript, and the font size. A two-line subtitle banner for the room screen. Full transcripts, summaries and subtitle files after the event.

What you get

Subtitles on every phone

Public or password-protected page per session. Attendees scan a QR code, choose their language and read. Nothing to install.

Subtitle banner for screens

A paced two-line banner made for the room screen or the video feed: readable speed, no rewriting under the reader's eyes. Also embeddable in your own web page.

Simultaneous translation

Several target languages per room at once. Attendees switch language on their own device. Used with 5 languages across 8 rooms at Wikimania 2026.

Made for AV control rooms

SRT and RTMP ingest, one channel per room, sessions scheduled in advance with automatic start and stop. Multi-room, multi-day.

Bots for online meetings

A LinTO bot joins Microsoft Teams, Jitsi, BigBlueButton or Visio (La Suite numérique), transcribes with the participants' names and feeds the same public page.

Hosted in France, or on your site

Our cloud, GPU included, or a deployment inside your infrastructure for sensitive sessions. Open-source stack in both cases.

Questions

What is the delay between speech and subtitles?

A few seconds. Text appears as the sentence is spoken and is finalized when it ends. Translations follow the finalized sentence, a few seconds later.

What does my AV provider need to send you?

One audio stream per room, SRT or RTMP, from the mixing desk or the video encoder. We give them the destination address and a stream id per room, and we test it together before the event. No hardware to rent.

Can attendees use it without an app?

Yes. The public page opens in any phone browser from a QR code or a link. It can be public, password-protected, or reserved to members of your organization.

Does it replace interpreters?

It gives rooms without interpretation a written translation, and it gives every room an accessible transcript. Wikimania 2026 used both: human interpreters in some rooms, LinTO subtitles in all of them.

Can it run inside our own network?

Yes. The full stack, GPU models included, deploys on your Kubernetes or Docker infrastructure. Sensitive sessions never leave your site.

What do we keep after the event?

Every session becomes a transcript in your LinTO Studio workspace: editable, with speakers, exportable as DOCX, PDF, SRT or VTT, and summarized on request.
